Our story

From two laptops to a full product team

My Web Studio started in a co-working corner with one rule: never hand over something you'd be embarrassed to maintain. That rule still shapes every pull request we review.

Over the years we've grown into a cross-functional team of strategists, designers and engineers — but we've deliberately stayed small enough that nothing gets lost in translation between sales, design and delivery.

  • Senior-only team — no juniors learning on your budget
  • Fixed-scope sprints with weekly demos and shared roadmaps
  • Open-source contributions and internal tooling days
  • Carbon-offset hosting for every site we launch

What we believe

Four values we actually use

Not poster slogans — these are the tie-breakers in our design reviews and retros. When two options look equally good, these decide.

01

Clarity over cleverness

If a stakeholder can't explain it to their CEO, we haven't finished.

02

Test, don't guess

Every major decision gets a hypothesis and a way to be proven wrong.

03

Accessible by default

WCAG 2.2 AA is the floor, not the stretch goal, on every build.

04

Leave it better

Cleaner code, documented systems and a team that can run it without us.

Milestones

Twelve years, briefly

2014

Two laptops, one rule

Ava and Daniel start My Web Studio out of a Pakistan co-working space, taking on front-end work nobody else wanted.

2017

First product team

We stop selling hours and start selling outcomes — designers, engineers and a strategist embedded together.

2020

Distributed by design

Remote-first processes built for three time zones. Clients get a four-hour overlap window, every day.

2023

Accessibility practice

A dedicated a11y discipline after our tenth WCAG audit — now baked into every sprint, not bolted on.

2026

34 specialists, still independent

Delivering work in nine countries while staying small enough that founders still join the kick-off call.
